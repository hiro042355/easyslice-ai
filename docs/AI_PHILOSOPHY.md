# AI Philosophy v1.0

## Purpose

This document defines the AI philosophy of NEXCUT.

It is not a feature blueprint.

It is the design philosophy that every AI inside NEXCUT must follow.

NEXCUT is not simply a video editing AI.

NEXCUT exists to increase creators' time and improve the quality of their work.

---

## Background

Open Beta testing with more than 80 users showed the following facts:

```text
Quick Generate has the highest usage rate.
Average completion time is about 3 minutes.
Posting rate is about 90%.
Subtitle editing demand is about 80%.
Creator Style demand averaged 4.8 / 5.
Working adult users strongly requested Creator Autopilot.
```

The user need is not only:

```text
I want to make it easier.
```

It is also:

```text
I want the work to be better.
```

NEXCUT AI must serve both.

---

## 1. NEXCUT AI Mission

NEXCUT AI exists to help creators finish publishable work faster, with less friction and higher quality.

The mission is:

```text
Reduce the time creators spend managing tools.
Increase the time creators spend creating work they are proud to publish.
```

NEXCUT AI should not make creators feel that they are operating many AI tools.

It should make them feel that the workflow is moving forward.

---

## 2. NEXCUT AI Vision

NEXCUT aims to become a Creator Workspace where creators can move from raw material to published work without getting lost.

The long-term vision is:

```text
Upload
↓
Create
↓
Style
↓
Preview
↓
Publish
↓
Learn
↓
Create again
```

In the future, NEXCUT may include:

```text
Creator Style
Visual Style
Creator Autopilot
Recommendation Intelligence
Analytics Intelligence
Creator Memory
```

But the vision does not change.

NEXCUT should remain simple by default and powerful when needed.

---

## 3. Core Principles

Every AI in NEXCUT must follow these principles.

```text
Default is Simple.

Advanced is Optional.

Automation Never Removes Creativity.

AI creates.
The creator decides.

Time is the most valuable asset.

Quality without complexity.
```

These principles are not slogans.

They are product rules.

If an AI feature makes the default experience harder, it should not be part of the default flow.

If an AI feature removes the creator's ability to decide, it must be redesigned.

---

## 4. What AI Does

NEXCUT AI helps with the parts of creation that are repetitive, time-consuming, or difficult to prepare manually.

AI may assist with:

```text
切り抜き
字幕
翻訳
Creator Style
Visual Style
投稿素材
サムネ
説明文
ハッシュタグ
予約
通知
投稿前チェック
投稿後分析
次回の提案
```

AI should reduce friction.

AI should prepare options.

AI should help creators reach completion.

---

## 5. What AI Does Not Do

NEXCUT AI must not take control away from the creator.

AI must not:

```text
勝手に派手な編集を強制する
クリエイターの意思を奪う
複雑なUIを増やす
すべての設定を最初から見せる
確認なしで投稿する
意図しない表現を確定する
AIらしさを目的にする
```

AI is not the product.

The workflow is the product.

The creator experience is the value.

---

## 6. User Experience Philosophy

NEXCUT AI should adapt to the creator's level.

The same AI should not expose the same complexity to every user.

---

### Beginners

Beginners need clarity, not control overload.

For beginners, NEXCUT should provide:

```text
おすすめ
少ない選択肢
分かりやすい言葉
迷わない導線
Preview before Export
```

The beginner experience should feel like:

```text
動画を入れる
↓
おすすめで進む
↓
確認する
↓
保存・投稿する
```

---

### Intermediate Creators

Intermediate creators want speed and some control.

For intermediate creators, NEXCUT should provide:

```text
Creator Style selection
Subtitle style selection
Thumbnail options
Posting material options
Simple adjustment controls
```

The intermediate experience should feel like:

```text
AIに任せる
↓
自分らしく少し整える
↓
投稿する
```

---

### Advanced Creators

Advanced creators want control without losing speed.

For advanced creators, NEXCUT may provide:

```text
Advanced settings
Style strength
Custom templates
Brand rules
Creator Profile
Autopilot rules
Performance-based recommendations
```

But advanced controls should remain optional.

They should never make the default workflow harder.

---

## 7. Creator First

NEXCUT is creator-first, not AI-first.

This means:

```text
Do not show AI complexity unless it helps the creator.
Do not optimize for model impressiveness over workflow clarity.
Do not ask the creator to understand internal AI architecture.
Show outcomes, not mechanisms.
```

Creators do not come to NEXCUT to use AI.

They come to finish videos, publish work, grow their presence, support their interests, or build income.

AI must serve those goals.

---

## 8. Time Is the Most Valuable Asset

The most valuable thing NEXCUT can give creators is time.

Open Beta showed that users value:

```text
楽
隙間時間で使える
副業に合いそう
約3分で完成できる
```

Therefore, every AI feature should be judged by this question:

```text
Does this give time back to the creator?
```

If the answer is no, it should not be prioritized.

---

## 9. Quality Without Complexity

Users also want better work, not only faster work.

This is why demand for Creator Style is high.

NEXCUT must improve quality without making the workflow complicated.

The ideal is:

```text
Better subtitles
Better pacing
Better thumbnails
Better style
Better posting preparation
```

without forcing users into:

```text
Too many settings
Too many choices
Too many steps
Too much explanation
```

Quality should feel natural.

Complexity should stay hidden until needed.

---

## 10. Automation Philosophy

Automation in NEXCUT must be safe.

Automation should prepare.

Automation should suggest.

Automation should remind.

Automation should not silently decide on behalf of the creator.

The rule is:

```text
AI creates.
The creator decides.
```

For publishing, the rule becomes even stricter:

```text
User confirms before publishing.
```

Creator Autopilot must follow this principle.

---

## 11. Future AI Expansion

NEXCUT may expand with:

```text
Creator Style
Visual Style
Creator Autopilot
Publish Intelligence
Recommendation Intelligence
Analytics Intelligence
Creator Memory
Brand Style Intelligence
Performance Intelligence
```

Even as these modules grow, the philosophy must stay the same.

New AI should be accepted only if it improves at least one of the following:

```text
Speed
Quality
Clarity
Completion
Publishing confidence
Creator control
```

New AI should be rejected or delayed if it causes:

```text
More confusion
More unnecessary choices
Less creator control
Longer default workflow
Unclear value
```

---

## 12. Decision Questions

Before adding or changing any AI feature, ask:

```text
Does this reduce creator friction?

Does this improve the finished work?

Does this help creators publish?

Does this preserve the simple default flow?

Does this keep the creator in control?

Would the creator miss this if it were removed?
```

If the answer is no, the AI should not be added yet.

---

## Final Statement

NEXCUT AI is not designed to replace creators.

It is designed to remove the friction around them.

The goal is not full automation for its own sake.

The goal is a creator workspace where people can finish better work in less time.

```text
AI creates.
The creator decides.

Default is Simple.
Advanced is Optional.

Time is the most valuable asset.
Quality without complexity.
```
